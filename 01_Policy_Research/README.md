# The Impact of 287(g) MOA on Immigrants' Educational Attainment
### Project Overview
This research explores the effect of immigration enforcement policies on the human capital development of immigrant communities. Specifically, it analyzes how the implementation of the **287(g) Memorandum of Agreement (MOA)**, which allows local law enforcement to act as federal immigration agents, affected immigrants' dropout rates.
### Methodology & Technical Stack
* **Econometric Framework:** Difference-in-Differences (DiD) approach to isolate the policy effect across different years.
* **Key Variables:**
    * Dependent: Educational attainment levels (dropout rates).
    * Policy Variable: Adoption of 287(g) agreements at the county level.
    * Controls: County fixed effects, year fixed effects.
* **Software:**
    * STATA: For data cleaning, merging county-level data, and running regression models.
    * LaTeX: For technical reporting and formatting of complex statistical tables.
### Key Insights
* The Effect: The study identifies a correlation between increased enforcement and a decline in dropout rates, likely due to a change in immigrant behavior due to heightened fear of deportation and decreased trust in public institutions.
* Implications: Beyond the immediate social impact, the research highlights the masked costs of declining dropout rates such as declining mental health in immigrant students, distrust in social institutions, and increased targeted interventions by schools.
* Strategic Takeaway: Proves that local policy shifts can have unintended, heterogeneous, far-reaching consequences on educational attainment, parental engagement, and social mobility.
